About the film

Produced by The Post Gen. Directed by Elizabeth Ai, the film weaves oral history, archival audio, and field recordings into a portrait of the music, fashion, and politics of post-1975 Vietnamese-American youth culture. The companion book — same title, longer arc — is in print now.
